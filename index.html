ArchiAR

Preview your model in 3D & AR

ArchiAR runs in your browser — no install. Here is the fastest path to a shareable preview.

  1. 1Explore the demo in 3D preview: drag to rotate on desktop, tilt your phone on mobile.
  2. 2Switch to AR. Print a Hiro marker or show it on another screen, then point your camera.
  3. 3Load your model: append ?model=URL to this page (GLB/GLTF, CORS-enabled).
Camera is used only for AR, locally on your device.
